Behaviour analysis: the part that's about you

Most training hands every player the same lessons. EloClimb's AI studies your own games and looks for what your mistakes have in common. Then it points your opening, middlegame, endgame and puzzle work at exactly that.

  • When it happens. Early, in the middlegame, or once the endgame starts.
  • What the clock says. Whether you slip with plenty of time or only in a scramble.
  • What kind of position. Open or closed, attacking or defending, ahead or behind.
  • Which tactic you miss. Forks, pins, back-rank mates, loose pieces.

From your games to your training plan

  1. 1.

    Play your games

    Keep playing the way you normally do. EloClimb works from what you actually do at the board, not from a quiz.

  2. 2.

    See your habits

    The AI goes through your games and reports the patterns in plain language, like the three sample players above.

  3. 3.

    Train what matters

    Your lessons and puzzles shift toward your weakest habits, and the report updates as you fix them.
